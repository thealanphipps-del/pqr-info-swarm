Title: SYSTEM AND METHOD FOR HIGH-FREQUENCY ACOUSTIC DATA TRANSMISSION
Patent Number: US2026102948A1
Inventor: Dr. Aris Thorne
Abstract: A system for transmitting structured data using high-frequency acoustic waves beyond the range of human hearing. The system includes a modulator for encoding digital signals into ultrasonic pulses and a receiver array capable of demodulating these signals in noisy environments.
Description: 
The present invention relates to data transmission over acoustic channels.
In one embodiment, the system uses frequencies between 18 kHz and 22 kHz.
The error correction module employs Reed-Solomon coding to prevent data loss.
Claims:
1. A system for data transmission comprising an acoustic emitter configured to transmit at 18-22 kHz.
2. The system of claim 1, further comprising a demodulator using Reed-Solomon error correction.
