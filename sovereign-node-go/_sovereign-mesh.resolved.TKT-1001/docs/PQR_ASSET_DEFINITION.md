# PQR Coin: Official Asset Definition

## Asset Metadata
*   **Name**: PQR Chain Token
*   **Symbol**: PQR
*   **Total Supply**: 1,000,000,000 (1 Billion)
*   **Type**: Layer-1 Governance & Gas Utility Token
*   **Purpose**: Official utility for all Sovereign City services, DNS/IP registration, and cross-mesh governance.

## Economic Utility
1.  **Gas Engine**: Required for every transaction and state-mutation on the PQR Chain.
2.  **Governance Utility**: Staking PQR is mandatory for voting on Sovereign DAO proposals.
3.  **Cross-Asset Liquidity**: PQR acts as the stable baseline for swapping internal mesh assets like SURFGO and GO.

## Compliance & Transparency
The PQR Foundation is committed to transparency. The distribution of PQR is governed by the 4/5 Quorum ledger, and all emissions are published in real-time to the public-facing `ledger.pqr.info` endpoint.
