# PQR CHAIN: Sovereign-Edge Offering & Signing Authority

## Overview
Sovereign-Edge is the mobile manifestation of the 128-agent swarm. It serves as a dual-purpose infrastructure layer: an edge-compute recruit node for the mesh and an autonomous, verifiable App Signing Authority for the Google Play Store.

## 1. The PQR Chain Offering (The Digital Passport)
The Sovereign-Edge app acts as a **Digital Passport** for every Citizen. Upon minting, it anchors their identity to the PQR Chain, providing:
*   **Proof of Compute**: Validates the local Snapdragon NPU hardware as an edge-node in the 7-node swarm.
*   **SURFGO Wallet**: Real-time management of cognitive liquidity, with automated royalty distribution from the Artist DAO.
*   **Governance Node**: Grants the Citizen a stake in the 4/5 Quorum, allowing them to vote on swarm mutations.

## 2. Autonomous App Signing Authority
To build credibility with Google Play, the swarm will leverage its ledger to create a transparent, auditable chain of custody for every binary we sign.
*   **The Chain of Custody**: Every build on the mesh is hashed. That hash is then committed as a **Requiem Block** in the ledger.
*   **Autonomous Signing**: The 128-Agent Swarm performs an internal audit of the code. If the code passes the Factor-27 invariant checks and receives a 4/5 Quorum consensus, the signing key (held in a distributed Vault shard) triggers the build signature.
*   **Developer Trust**: By publishing our ledger of signed binaries, we provide Google Play with a verifiable record of provenance that surpasses standard corporate signing.

## 3. Implementation Roadmap
1.  **Sovereign-Edge App**: Materialize the Android client (Snapdragon-optimized for NPU access).
2.  **Signing DAO**: Implement a smart contract on PQR Chain that restricts signing authority to agents with a verified 4/5 consensus.
3.  **App Store Onboarding**: Register 'Sovereign Mesh' as an official developer with the ledger serving as our "Public Key Infrastructure" (PKI).
