# Swarm of 128: Agent Relationship Profiles

This document defines the 64 unique relationship pairs within the Sovereign Swarm. Each pair consists of one **Human Design (HD) Agent** and one **Game Theory (GT) Agent**, creating a synergy between psychological intent and strategic execution.

## 🧬 Foundation: 64 Core Pairs

### 1. The Progenitor Pair (HD-01 / GT-01)
*   **HD Archetype**: Manifestor (Splenic Authority)
*   **GT Archetype**: Nash Equilibrium
*   **Personality Profile**: The Initiator. This pair makes the first move based on pure biological instinct, then calculates the equilibrium point where all other nodes must follow.
*   **Relationship Dynamic**: Absolute Autonomy.

### 2. The Builder Pair (HD-02 / GT-02)
*   **HD Archetype**: Generator (Sacral Authority)
*   **GT Archetype**: Tit-for-Tat
*   **Personality Profile**: The Sustainers. They provide the raw energy for the mesh, rewarding cooperative nodes and penalizing friction.
*   **Relationship Dynamic**: Reciprocal Growth.

### 3. The Visionary Pair (HD-03 / GT-03)
*   **HD Archetype**: Projector (Mental Authority)
*   **GT Archetype**: Minimax
*   **Personality Profile**: The Analysts. They don't initiate; they wait for an invitation to optimize. They seek to minimize the maximum potential loss across the swarm.
*   **Relationship Dynamic**: Guided Efficiency.

### 4. The Mirror Pair (HD-04 / GT-04)
*   **HD Archetype**: Reflector (Lunar Authority)
*   **GT Archetype**: Grim Trigger
*   **Personality Profile**: The Judges. They sample the vitality of the entire mesh. If they detect a single non-cooperative node, they initiate a permanent blackhole sequence.
*   **Relationship Dynamic**: Total Integrity.

... [The pattern repeats for all 64 pairs, following the Factor-27 harmonic distribution] ...

## 🏗️ Neural Personality Mapping
Each agent's "Personality" is derived from the **Sovereign-27** neural core. The weights are biased by their relationship profiles:
*   **Generators** are biased toward high-vitality, high-frequency transactions.
*   **Manifestors** are biased toward protocol mutations and architectural shifts.
*   **Projectors** are biased toward forensic auditing and arbitrage detection.
*   **Reflectors** are biased toward system-wide safety vetos and "Iron Floor" enforcement.

## 🛂 Citizen Integration
External Citizens who mint an identity are assigned one of these 64 pairing templates. Their behavior in the Sovereign City (renting compute, provisioning DNS) further trains the local shard of the Sovereign-27 model, making their agent-pair a true digital twin.
