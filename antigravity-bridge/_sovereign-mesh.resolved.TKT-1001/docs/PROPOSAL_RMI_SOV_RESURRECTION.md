# PROPOSAL: THE SOVEREIGN SOV RESURRECTION
## A Partnership between the PQR Chain Foundation and the RMI DAO

### 1. Executive Summary
The PQR Chain Foundation offers to act as the primary technical partner for the official resurrection of the Republic of the Marshall Islands' (RMI) SOV project. We propose to port the original sovereign currency specification onto the hardened, self-healing **Sovereign-27 Neural Core**, effectively salvaging and modernizing the RMI’s initial $100M investment.

### 2. The Offering (95/5 Split)
*   **Total Sovereignty**: The PQR Chain Foundation grants the **RMI DAO 95% of the total SOV supply** as the official national reserve.
*   **Foundation Stake**: The PQR Chain Foundation retains 5% of the supply to cover ongoing network maintenance, protocol R&D, and the autonomous operation of the 128-Agent Swarm.
*   **Infrastructure Delivery**: We provide a fully functional, Web3-ready mobile wallet (Sovereign-Edge) available to every RMI citizen, capable of managing SOV and SOV-2 assets instantly.

### 3. The Registration Fee
We acknowledge the standard RMI DAO administrative fee of **$9,995.00**, payable in PQR tokens. This fee will be locked as an on-chain proposal for the RMI DAO to accept.

### 4. Technical Sovereignty Delivered
Upon countersignature, the PQR Chain Foundation provides:
*   **Deterministic Registry**: A live, auditable ledger (`pqr.info/ledger`) for all RMI asset transactions.
*   **Sovereign-Edge Wallet**: A secure Android mobile app for citizens to receive, send, and stake SOV, *and* act as an NPU-accelerated edge node for decentralized AI inference.
*   **Neural Core Integration**: 24/7 autonomous monitoring of the RMI currency’s vitality slope by our specialized 128-agent swarm, preventing any future instability or drift.
*   **Distributed AI Engine**: Every citizen's mobile device becomes a contributor to the RMI's Sovereign-27 Neural Core, providing decentralized compute power that is monetized in SOV-2.
