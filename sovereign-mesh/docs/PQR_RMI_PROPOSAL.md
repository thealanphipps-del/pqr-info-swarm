We propose to provide the RMI DAO with a full-stack, autonomous digital registration infrastructure. This system will serve as the official digital registry for the following:
*   **TLD Registrar Service**: Restoration and operation of the abandoned `.mh` (Marshall Islands) Top-Level Domain.
*   **IP Registry**: Global, immutable registration for Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights on the PQR Chain.
*   **Legal Identity Registry**: Cryptographic "Passport" infrastructure for digital citizens/entities recognized by the RMI DAO.
